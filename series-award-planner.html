Before you book — the fine print

Cuba · OFAC
Book Havana with non-US currencies (Avios / Flying Blue) and route via Europe or Istanbul. Every cash business option goes through the US, which means ESTA + a declared OFAC travel category. Avoid it entirely by flying the award.
Venezuela · entry
Turkish IST–Caracas is the clean non-US routing. Check current visa rules and travel advisories close to departure — this one moves.
Haiti · gateway reality
Fly into Cap-Haïtien (CAP), not Port-au-Prince (effectively closed on security/FAA grounds). No premium cabin exists on the final hop — Sunrise Airways in economy from SDQ / HAV / Miami.
