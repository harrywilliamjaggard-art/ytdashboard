# Project & Personal Notes

## Travel preferences (flights & hotels)

- **Hotel loyalty — prioritise Hilton.** When searching for or recommending
  hotels, always prioritise the **Hilton** chain (Hilton, Hilton Garden Inn,
  DoubleTree, Hampton, Waldorf Astoria, Conrad, Canopy, Curio, Tapestry,
  Embassy Suites, Home2, Homewood Suites, Motto, Tempo, Signia, LXR, etc.).
  Reason: actively collecting **Hilton Honors points**.
  - Surface Hilton options first, and call out when booking direct with Hilton
    (vs. an OTA) is needed to actually earn points and status credit.
  - Still show a non-Hilton option when it's dramatically better on price or
    location, but flag the points trade-off so the choice is explicit.

- **No fixed home airport — nomadic.** Don't assume an origin city; ask or
  infer from context. Preferred **connecting hubs: Denpasar (DPS), Dubai (DXB),
  Doha (DOH)** — route through these when it's sensible.

- **Cabin: business class**, and **strongly prefers redeeming miles/points**
  over cash. Always weigh award value against the cash fare and call out the
  cents-per-point so the trade-off is explicit.

- **Miles & points currencies collected** (map redemptions to these; balances
  change, so confirm before booking):
  - **Avios** (British Airways / Iberia / Qatar / oneworld) — primary currency.
    Both **Revolut** points and **UK Amex Membership Rewards** transfer in, so
    treat them as feeding one big Avios pool.
  - **Etihad Guest** — large balance; best for Etihad/Gulf (DXB/AUH) long-haul,
    weak for the Americas.
  - **Singapore KrisFlyer** — Star Alliance (Turkish, Avianca, Copa, SIA);
    natural fit for DPS/Asia routings.
  - Hub → currency pairing: **DOH → Qatar Qsuite via Avios**; **DXB → Etihad
    Guest**; **DPS → KrisFlyer (Star Alliance)**.
  - For **Cuba**, redeem with **non-US** programs (Avios/Flying Blue) to avoid
    OFAC friction.
